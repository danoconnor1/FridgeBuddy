# Fridge Buddy

A web app to track items in your fridge and get recipe suggestions based on what you have.

## Features

- Track fridge items with expiration dates
- Get alerts for items expiring soon
- Store favorite recipes with ingredients
- Get recipe suggestions based on available items
- Fully persistent — data saves to your device
- Works on mobile (add to home screen on iPhone)

## How to use

1. Add items to your fridge with expiration dates
2. Add your favorite recipes and their ingredients
3. Get instant suggestions for recipes you can make
4. Be notified when items are about to expire

## Installation

Just open the URL in your browser. To add to iPhone home screen:

1. Open in Safari
2. Tap the Share button
3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
4. Name it "Fridge Buddy"
5. It appears as an app on your home screen

## Local storage

All your data is saved in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent to a server. Your data persists even if you close the app.
